For millions of people in Nigeria and Ghana, crypto isn't a speculative hobby — it's how they get paid by overseas clients, protect savings from currency swings and send money home. But the gap between owning crypto and actually using it day to day is still wider than it should be.

This guide walks through what you need before you start, the exact steps, and the habits that keep your funds safe.

## Before you start

You'll need a verified Vent account, a bank account in your own name, and the wallet that currently holds your crypto. Verification usually takes a few minutes and only needs to happen once.

- A government-issued ID that matches your bank account name
- Access to the wallet or exchange where your coins are stored
- Two-factor authentication switched on in the Vent app

## Step by step

Open the app, choose the asset you want to sell and enter an amount. You'll see the exact rate and what lands in your bank before you confirm — no hidden spread, no surprise fees.

> “The best crypto product is the one you forget is crypto. You should just see your money arrive.”
>
> — Vent product team

## Keeping your funds safe

Never share your one-time codes, even with someone claiming to be from Vent support. We will never ask for them. Double-check wallet addresses by comparing the first and last six characters, and start with a small test transfer when sending to a new address.
